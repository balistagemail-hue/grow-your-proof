1. What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files that a website stores in your browser. They can remember things like your preferences, keep you logged in or measure how a site is used. Cookies set by the website you are visiting are called first-party cookies. Cookies set by another company, for example an analytics or advertising provider, are called third-party cookies.

Cookies can be session cookies, which disappear when you close your browser, or persistent cookies, which stay on your device until they expire or you delete them.

5. How long cookies stay on your device

Session cookies are removed when you close your browser. Persistent cookies stay until their expiry date or until you delete them. Since we don't set analytics or advertising cookies, nothing from us follows you around after your visit.
