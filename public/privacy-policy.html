Privacy policy

Last updated: 21 October 2026

This policy explains how Grow Your Proof handles your personal data when you visit growyourproof.com, fill in our contact form, book a call with us or work with us as a client. We keep it short and only collect what we need to help you grow your app.

1. Who we are

Grow Your Proof is a digital marketing agency for AI and tech apps, based in the Netherlands. We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. If you have any question about your privacy, email us at Partner@growyourproof.com.

In this policy, "we", "us" and "our" mean Grow Your Proof. "You" means the person visiting our website or contacting us, or the company on whose behalf that person acts. "Website" means growyourproof.com.

2. What we collect

Information you give us

When you fill in our "Get in touch" form, we ask for:

  • your name, company or app name, email address and phone number;
  • details about your app, such as its stage, the services you are interested in and your monthly marketing budget.

When you book a call, the booking page asks for your name and email address so we can send you an invitation. If you email or call us, we receive whatever you choose to share in that message.

Information we receive automatically

Like every website, our hosting provider automatically processes technical data when you visit, such as your IP address, browser type, device type, the pages you visit and the time of your visit. This is used to deliver the site and keep it secure, not to build a profile of you.

Client and campaign data

If you become a client, we also process the information needed to run your campaigns, such as contact details of your team, invoices, and the ad and performance data you give us access to.

3. Cookies

Our website does not use advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not use analytics tools that follow you across websites. Our fonts are loaded from Google Fonts, which means your browser connects to Google's servers and shares your IP address with Google when the page loads.

The booking page is run by Google Calendar. When you open it, Google's own cookies and privacy policy apply. If we ever add analytics or marketing cookies, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first where the law requires it.

4. How we use your information, and why we are allowed to

  • To reply to your request and plan a call. We use your form answers to prepare for the discovery call and to contact you by email or phone. Legal basis: steps taken at your request before entering into a contract.
  • To deliver our services. To run UGC campaigns, ads and the other services we agreed on. Legal basis: performance of a contract.
  • To send relevant updates. We may occasionally email you about services similar to what you asked about. You can opt out at any time with one reply. Legal basis: legitimate interest.
  • To run and protect our website. Technical data helps us keep the site online and secure. Legal basis: legitimate interest.
  • To meet legal obligations. For example, keeping invoices for tax purposes. Legal basis: legal obligation.

We never sell your personal data.

5. Who we share it with

We only share your data with parties that help us run our business, and only as much as they need:

  • Service providers such as our website host (Vercel), email and calendar provider (Google Workspace / Google Calendar) and form provider. They process data on our behalf and may not use it for their own purposes.
  • Creators we work with, but only the briefing information needed to make content for your app, never your personal contact details unless you agree.
  • Authorities, when we are legally required to.
  • A buyer or successor, if our business is ever merged or sold, under the same protections as this policy.
  • Anyone else, only with your consent.

6. How long we keep it

We keep your data only as long as we need it. If you contact us but don't become a client, we delete your form answers within 12 months of our last contact. Client data is kept for the duration of our collaboration, and invoices and related records for 7 years, as Dutch tax law requires. After that, we delete the data or keep it only in a form that can no longer be traced back to you.

7. International transfers

Some of our service providers, such as Google and Vercel, may process data outside the European Economic Area, for example in the United States. When that happens, we rely on safeguards recognised under the GDPR, such as the EU–US Data Privacy Framework or the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

8. Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your data against loss, unauthorised access or misuse. Access is limited to team members who need it for their work. No online service is 100% secure, but if a data breach ever affects you, we will notify you and the Dutch Data Protection Authority where the law requires it.

9. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the following rights. To use any of them, email Partner@growyourproof.com. We may ask you to confirm your identity first, and we will respond within one month.

  • Access: ask what data we hold about you and get a copy.
  • Correction: ask us to fix data that is wrong or incomplete.
  • Deletion: ask us to delete your data, unless we must keep it by law.
  • Restriction: ask us to temporarily stop using your data.
  • Objection: object to processing based on our legitimate interest, including marketing emails.
  • Portability: receive the data you gave us in a common format, or have it sent to another party.
  • Withdraw consent: where we rely on your consent, you can withdraw it at any time.

You also have the right to file a complaint with the Dutch Data Protection Authority (Autoriteit Persoonsgegevens) or the supervisory authority in your own country. We'd appreciate the chance to sort it out with you first.

10. Children

Our services are meant for businesses and are not aimed at anyone under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

11. Other websites

Our website links to other sites, such as the Google booking page and our social media. We are not responsible for how those sites handle your data, so we recommend reading their privacy policies.

12. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The latest version is always on this page, with the date it was last updated at the top.
