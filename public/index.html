Frequently asked questions

What services does Grow Your Proof offer?

Everything an AI or tech app needs to grow with UGC: content ideas and scripts, hooks built to stop the scroll, creator sourcing and management, filming and editing, ad management on Meta and TikTok, App Store optimization and monthly performance reviews. One team handles it from the first idea to the ad that scales.

Why does UGC work so well for AI and tech apps?

People trust people. A real person showing how an app fits into their day explains it faster and more convincingly than a polished brand video. That trust is what turns a view into a download, and it is why UGC usually outperforms classic ads for apps.

How do you find the right creators for my app?

We match creators to your audience, niche and tone of voice. Every creator gets a clear brief and script, and we direct and manage them from first shoot to final edit, so you never have to chase anyone.

Do you also run our ads?

Yes. We launch your videos on Meta and TikTok, test different hooks against each other and scale the winners. You keep full control of your ad account and budget.

How do you measure results?

We track the numbers that matter for your app: views, engagement, installs, cost per install and return on ad spend. Every month we walk you through what worked, what didn't and where we focus next.

Who owns the videos?

You get the usage rights to use every video in your ads and on your own channels. We arrange this with the creators upfront, so you can use the content without surprises.

How fast can we get started?

After a short discovery call we build your custom strategy plan. Your first videos are ready within 10 working days after kickoff.

Do you only work with AI apps?

We focus on AI and tech apps, because that is where we know what works. Building a different kind of app? Get in touch and we'll tell you honestly whether we're the right fit.
