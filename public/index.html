Frequently asked questions

What services does Grow Your Proof offer?

Video ads and paid social for licensed fintech, trading and investing apps: compliance-first scripts and hooks, studio-shot videos with many different faces, ads management on Meta and TikTok, performance analysis, App Store optimization and website design. One team handles it from the first idea to the campaign that scales.

Why do you only work with licensed financial apps?

Finance has strict advertising rules and the platforms check them. We only work with advertisers that hold the licence or registration their market requires, because that protects your ad accounts, your brand and ours.

Do you give investment advice or promise returns?

No. We never give personal investment advice and we never promise profits. Our videos explain how your app works and carry the risk warning your market requires. Your compliance team approves every script before we film.

Who appears in the videos?

Presenters and creators we film in our studio, in professional or UGC style, with different ages and backgrounds so you can test which face and message work best. We agree usage rights with every presenter upfront. They explain your app and never claim personal results they did not have.

Do you also run our ads?

Yes. We launch your videos on Meta and TikTok, test different hooks against each other and scale the winners. You keep full control of your ad account and budget, and your licence and the platforms' approvals stay with you.

How do you measure results?

We track the numbers that matter for your app: views, cost per new account, completed identity checks and, where your data allows, funded accounts and return on ad spend. Every month we walk you through what worked, what did not and where we focus next.

Who owns the videos?

You get the usage rights to use every video in your ads and on your own channels. We arrange this with the presenters upfront, so you can use the content without surprises.

How fast can we get started?

After a short discovery call we build your strategy plan. Once your compliance team has signed off on the scripts, we schedule the studio shoot. We agree the timeline with you in the first call.

Do you only work with fintech?

Yes. We focus on licensed fintech, trading and investing apps, because strict rules and expensive customers need a specialist. Building something else? Get in touch and we will tell you honestly whether we are the right fit.
